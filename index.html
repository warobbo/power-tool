    How to use

    1. Choose a preset if you want a head start, or keep the defaults.
    2. Tick only the appliances you will run, then set watts from the label or manual.
    3. Enter hours of use per day. For a fridge, hours means compressor run-time, not time switched on.
    4. Totals update as you type. Refresh the page: your numbers stay on this device.

    FAQ

    • Wh vs Ah. Watt-hours (Wh) measure energy. Amp-hours (Ah) are that energy divided by battery voltage.
    • 12 V vs 24 V. The same Wh is half the Ah on a 24 V system (Wh ÷ 24) compared with 12 V (Wh ÷ 12).
    • The fridge is often the big load. A compressor fridge can use more energy per day than lights, phones and a laptop combined.
    • This is an estimate. Duty cycle, weather, age of kit and inverter efficiency all change the real figure.
